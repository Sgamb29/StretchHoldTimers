About The Stretch Hold Timers Site

Home

A collection of timers that make it easy to time stretches. No need to click to silence an alarm, simply press one button when you're ready to start a stretch. Displays the countdown of time left and indicates the finish with a short sound and color change.

It's possible to see improvements in flexibility with as little as 5 minutes of stretching per day! Holding each stretch for 20-60 seconds is recommended.

Fun stretching: Try listening to an audiobook, music or watching tv while stretching. And remember, be gentle with yourself in the poses.
